Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you visit The Offering God Can Accept website, published by Bradley Hobbs. The website provides written teaching and author information. The site does not currently offer user accounts, a contact form, or a feature for visitors to submit personal information.

Information Collected Automatically

The website itself does not intentionally collect personal information through forms or account registration. The hosting provider, GitHub Pages, may process technical information such as IP address, browser details, device information, and request logs as part of delivering and securing the website. Please review GitHub's privacy information for details about its practices.

Third-Party Services

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts. When your browser requests those fonts, Google may receive technical information, including your IP address and browser request data. Third-party services operate under their own privacy policies and practices.

The website may link to external websites. Bradley Hobbs does not control the privacy practices, content, or security of external sites. Review the applicable policies before providing information to another website.

Cookies and Tracking

This website does not intentionally set advertising cookies or use its own analytics or behavioral advertising tools. Hosting or third-party services may use technical processes necessary to provide their services. Browser settings allow you to manage or block cookies, although some third-party functionality may be affected.

Children's Privacy

This website is intended as a general audience teaching and author-information resource. The site does not knowingly request or collect personal information from children. A parent or guardian who believes a child has submitted personal information in connection with this website may contact the site owner using an appropriate public contact channel.

Policy Changes

This policy may be updated when the website's features or practices change. The effective date above will be revised when an update is published. Continued use of the website after an update means you are viewing the revised policy.

Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, use the public contact information provided by Bradley Hobbs through his author or professional website.

This policy is a general website disclosure, not legal advice. The wording should be reviewed and updated before adding forms, mailing lists, analytics, advertising, embedded media, or other data-collection features.
