About the Author

Bradley Hobbs is an author, educator, and writer whose work explores faith, healing, leadership, obedience, and the struggles people often experience within their spiritual journey.

His writing is shaped by a desire to speak to people who have felt misunderstood, overlooked, corrected without being heard, or pushed away when they were searching for God.

Through his writing, Bradley seeks to encourage readers to look beyond outward appearances and examine the condition of the heart.

The Offering God Can Accept

The Offering God Can Accept explores the difference between simply giving something to God and completely surrendering ourselves to Him.

Through the biblical accounts of Cain and Abel and Abraham and Isaac, the teaching examines faith, obedience, humility, correction, leadership, and the importance of remaining sensitive to the Spirit.

The message is ultimately a call to surrender.

God is not merely interested in what comes from our hands. He is interested in what comes from our hearts.

A Message for Leaders

Leadership requires more than having a position.

It requires humility.

A leader must remain willing to listen, examine their heart, receive correction, and allow God to reveal areas that need to change.

Not every criticism is correct, but every surrendered heart should be willing to ask:

“Lord, is there something You are trying to show me?”

Being corrected does not make a leader weak.

Being teachable allows a leader to continue growing.
